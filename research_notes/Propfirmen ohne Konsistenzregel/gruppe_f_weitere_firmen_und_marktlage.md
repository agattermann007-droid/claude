## Key Question 1: Which other active forex/CFD prop firms advertise models without any consistency rule, and which related profit-distribution rules apply?

### Takeaway
Outside the covered list, the only additional forex/CFD firms found with a clear "no consistency rule" model plus MT5 are FunderPro (Classic program: no consistency rule in the phases or when funded) and Pipstone Capital (no consistency rule, but a position-stacking rule).
