## 2. City Traders Imperium (CTI): consistency status and EA-relevant facts per model

### Takeaway
CTI's own site states "no hidden consistency rules" and no time limits. It says every program has balance-based drawdown, and it trades on MT5. It allows the trader's own EAs, but not third-party EAs or martingale. News trading and weekend holding are allowed on every program, and CTI mentions VPS solutions. **All four CTI models (1-Step, 2-Step, Instant Funding, Direct Funding) are therefore "no consistency rule" candidates according to official marketing.** Some review sites contradict this, and the 1-Step rules, XAUUSD/NAS100 availability and prices could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
**Consistency**
- "No time limits and no hidden consistency rules". CTI argues that most traders fail "because the rules force them to trade against their natural style, not because their strategy is bad". — [CTI home](https://citytradersimperium.com/); [CTI funded account challenge](https://citytradersimperium.com/funded-account-challenge-funding-program/) [official snippet]
- "Every program includes balance-based drawdown, no time limits, payouts any day including weekends, scaling up to $4M, and CTI Academy." — [CTI home](https://citytradersimperium.com/) [official snippet]
- CTI "rewards consistency and longevity" through a VIP program with Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers. — [CTI VIP program & payout enhancements](https://citytradersimperium.com/vip-program-payout/) [official snippet]
- **Conflict.** Review sites disagree:
  - Some describe a CTI "consistency rule": steady performance, no "large, high-risk gambling trades", and consistency "affecting eligibility for scaling or higher profit shares".
  - Another source states "there are no consistency rules on any challenge or instant funding program".
  - Sources: [LuxAlgo](https://www.luxalgo.com/prop-firms/city-traders-imperium/); [TradingFinder](https://tradingfinder.com/props/city-traders-imperium/rules/); [TradingFunder](https://tradingfunder.com/city-traders-imperium-rules/) [unverified (secondary source); the search tool merged these sites, so the claims could not be attributed to individual sites]

### Inferences
- According to CTI's own statements, 1-Step, 2-Step, Instant Funding and Direct Funding have no consistency rule in either the evaluation or the funded phase.
  - The VIP tiers "reward" consistency, but they look like an upside (higher split, on-demand payouts), not a payout-blocking rule.
  - Because secondary sites disagree, the T&C should be checked before buying.
- A self-coded or customised MT5 EA running on a VPS appears compatible with CTI's rules. A purchased (third-party) EA is not, and martingale or grid-style recovery logic is prohibited.
- Balance-based drawdown would favour an EA that holds floating losses, if it means open P/L is not counted. That definition is inferred from the marketing wording and could not be read in the rules.
- For an EA, Instant Funding (no daily DD, 6% static) and 2-Step (5%/10% balance-based) look structurally attractive. Instant Funding starts at only a 50% split.

## 4. Finotive Funding: consistency status and EA-relevant facts per model

### Takeaway
Finotive officially says **Instant Funding has no consistency rule**, and review sites say the **standard Challenge/funded accounts** have none either. **Finotive Pro** (the salary accounts) has a funded-phase trade-consistency rule and a quarterly 5% profit target:
- after 30 days, weekly trade count and volume must stay within ±25% of a baseline;
- the 5% profit target applies per 90 days.

All accounts are subject to Minimum Profitable Days (≥0.5% per day), a notional-volume cap, cash-risk rules and stop-loss rules. Only self-built EAs are allowed. MT5, VPS and gold/NAS100 availability could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
**Consistency**
- **Pro:** [Finotive Pro](https://finotivefunding.com/pro); [Pro Targets and Risk Limits](https://help.finotivefunding.com/en/articles/650-pro-targets-and-risk-limits) [official snippet]
  - "Pro accounts have additional rules which must be followed when funded such as the quarterly profit target and consistency rule."
  - After "30 paid days to trade without restrictions to establish a baseline", traders "must maintain their trade quantity and volume within ±25% of their baseline week to week".
  - Failing these rules "will NOT result in account failure, however the account will be demoted to a standard challenge account".
- **Pro details:** [TheTrustedProp](https://thetrustedprop.com/prop-firms/finotive-funding); [Lune](https://lunefi.com/blog/finotive-funding-an-in-depth-guide-rules-review-and-discount) [unverified (secondary source)]
  - At least 5% total profit every 90 days, counting withdrawn payouts and unrealised profit.
  - The baseline is computed from the challenge phase plus the first 30 funded days.
  - Example given: a baseline of 10 trades and 10 lots per week allows "7 to 12 trades and 7.5 to 12.5 lots".
- **Pro perks:** a monthly salary of 1% of purchased capital, accrued daily. A breach means "automatic downgrade to a standard funded account of the same size, with permanent loss of salary and 100% profit split". — [Finotive Pro (salary page)](https://finotivefunding.com/finotive-pro) [official snippet]
- **Instant Funding:** "no consistency is required on Instant Funding accounts. Traders are welcome to try different strategies, trade different instruments, or execution methods as they please." — [Finotive FAQ](https://finotivefunding.com/faq); [Finotive Instant Funding](https://finotivefunding.com/instant-funding) [official snippet]
- **Standard accounts:** "Finotive Funding does not enforce a consistency rule on standard accounts." — [TheTrustedProp](https://thetrustedprop.com/prop-firms/finotive-funding); [TradingFinder](https://tradingfinder.com/props/finotive-funding/rules/) [unverified (secondary source)]

**Related rules that act on profit distribution or trading style**
- **Minimum Profitable Day (MPD):** a trading day on which the balance at 17:00 New York time exceeds the previous day's closing balance by at least 0.5% of the initial balance. "You cannot progress, request payouts, or scale until MPD is met." — [Finotive help: MPD](https://help.finotivefunding.com/en/articles/929-what-is-a-minimum-profitable-day) [official snippet]
- **Notional Volume Limit:** caps the total notional value of open positions relative to balance. Example: a $50,000 account with a 2,000% limit can hold at most $1,000,000 notional. Each breach issues a "strike". — [Finotive help: notional volume](https://help.finotivefunding.com/en/articles/60-what-is-notional-volume-and-why-does-it-matter) [official snippet]
- **Drawdowns:** [Finotive help: daily drawdown](https://help.finotivefunding.com/en/articles/57-what-is-the-daily-drawdown-rule); [Finotive rules](https://finotivefunding.com/rules) [official snippet]
  - The daily loss limit resets at 17:00 New York time, based on the balance at that moment. Example with 4.5%: a $105,000 close means the equity floor is $100,275.
  - Example max overall drawdown of 9%, static from the initial balance: a $100,000 account has an equity floor of $91,000.
  - Both breaches are "hard breaches". Which model uses 4.5%/9% is not stated in the snippet.
- **Rule overhaul:** dated 3 Feb 2025 ("A New Era of Fair & Transparent Trading: Finotive Funding's Rule Updates Explained"). — [Finotive blog](https://blog.finotivefunding.com/2025/02/03/a-new-era-of-fair-transparent-trading-finotive-fundings-rule-updates-explained/) [title only]

**EA**
- EAs "are permitted if you designed, customized, or coded them yourself. Pre-purchased or mass-market 'plug-and-play' EAs are not allowed". All EAs must comply with Finotive's rules on drawdowns, cash risk, notional volume, stop losses and MPD. — [Finotive help: Can I use EAs?](https://help.finotivefunding.com/en/articles/70-can-i-use-expert-advisors-eas) [official snippet]

**Payouts**
- "Up to 100% reward split"; "Instant Funding with Up to 100% Profit Split". — [Finotive promo](https://promo.finotivefunding.com/); [Finotive rules](https://finotivefunding.com/rules) [official snippet]
- A help article on the minimum withdrawal amount exists, but its content was not returned. — [Finotive help](https://help.finotivefunding.com/en/articles/57-what-is-the-minimum-amount-i-can-withdraw) [title only]

### Inferences
- Candidates without a consistency rule:
  - **Finotive Instant Funding**, per the official statement.
  - **Finotive standard Challenge and its funded account**, per secondary sources.
- **Finotive Pro** is excluded: it has a trade-count and volume consistency rule in the funded phase.
- The MPD requirement (0.5%/day) is a "profitable day" rule. It is not a best-day cap, but it can delay progression and payouts for a low-volatility EA.
- The mandatory stop-loss, cash-risk and notional-volume rules constrain EA design, for example no SL-less grid logic and no large lot sizes on gold or NAS100.

## 6. Crypto Fund Trader (CFT): consistency status and EA-relevant facts per model

### Takeaway

## 7. Lux Trading Firm: consistency status and EA-relevant facts
